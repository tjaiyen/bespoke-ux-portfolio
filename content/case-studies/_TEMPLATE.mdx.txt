Authoring template for content/case-studies/<slug>.mdx
======================================================

Copy this file to `<slug>.mdx` (the `.txt` extension keeps the template itself out of the
loader, which only reads `*.mdx`). Frontmatter must match the Zod schema exactly —
src/lib/caseStudySchema.ts — or the build fails with the offending field named.

Before setting `ndaSanitized: true`, complete the NDA redaction checklist in the vault
(05-Checklists/Checklist-NDA-Redaction.md): anonymize the client, scrub or relativize
confidential metrics, run the blocklist grep, THEN flip the flag. The flag is an assertion
that you did the work, not a switch that does it.

Field notes:
  slug                  must equal the filename and match /^[a-z0-9-]+$/
  client                already anonymized — the blocklist scan double-checks
  userResearchSummary   >= 50 chars, substantive (70/30 rule, enforced by Zod)
  tradeOffAnalysis      >= 50 chars, must name a discarded direction and its cost
  businessImpactMetrics >= 1 entry; feeds <MetricsGrid />
  heroImage             must start with /images/ and exist under public/images/<slug>/
  published             false withholds the study from routes WITHOUT breaking the build
  ndaSanitized          must be literally true; anything else fails the build

## Problem

Context, scale, cross-functional dynamics, and your specific contribution. Two to three
paragraphs. State what was actually at stake in the business, not just the UI.

## Research

Expand userResearchSummary: methods, sample, synthesis artifacts, and how findings
reshaped the brief. This section plus Trade-offs must outweigh widget content (70/30).

## Trade-offs

Expand tradeOffAnalysis: at least one discarded direction, the decision criteria, and the
cost you accepted. This is the section senior reviewers read closest.

## Outcome

<MetricsGrid metrics={[
  { label: "Metric name", value: "+0.0%", change: "vs baseline", isPositive: true }
]} />

## Retrospective

What you would do differently. Candid reflection signals seniority; a flawless narrative
signals a rewritten one.
