Labour variance decomposition

A labour miss is two different problems wearing one number.

Decompose splits every labour variance into the part you paid more per hour for and the part you used more hours for. A $360 miss that reads as one number is $180 of rate and $180 of efficiency — one is a negotiation and the other is a line problem, and they are not fixed by the same person.

01 One rectangle — hours times rate

The oldest decomposition in cost accounting

Price times quantity. It is not sophisticated, and almost nobody actually does it by cost centre.

  • Rate is a negotiation

    Paying above standard per hour is a purchasing and scheduling question — overtime, premium shifts, agency labour — and it is answered away from the plant floor.

  • Efficiency is a line

    Using more hours than the standard allows is a throughput, changeover or downtime question, and it is answered by the people standing next to the machine.

  • By cost centre, not in total

    A plant-level variance of nearly zero routinely hides one centre badly over and another badly under, so the split is shown per centre as small multiples.

  • The joint variance, named

    When both rate and quantity move, the overlap belongs somewhere explicit rather than being quietly absorbed into whichever term is reported first.
