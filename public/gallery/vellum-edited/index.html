Sound engine

Signal flows from a pair of wavetable oscillators, through a self-oscillating ladder filter, and out via a rack of studio-grade effects — a complete voice from note-on to master bus.

  • Dual wavetable oscillators

    Two independent tables you can scan, morph, and sync — layered for thick unisons or detuned into shimmering movement.

  • Resonant ladder filter

    A zero-delay-feedback ladder with silky resonance that self-oscillates into a clean sine and drives into warm saturation.

  • Built-in studio effects

    Hall reverb, tape delay, chorus, and drive on board — mix a finished, mastered patch without ever leaving the panel.

Built for the sound in your head

Three morphable wavetable oscillators feed a resonant analog-modeled filter, wrapped in a modulation matrix deep enough to lose an afternoon in — and immediate enough to play live.

  • Living wavetables

    Scan through 128 hand-designed frames per table, or drop in your own single-cycle waveforms.

  • Warm analog filter

    A zero-delay-feedback ladder that self-oscillates cleanly and drives into gentle saturation.

  • Deep modulation

    Six LFOs, four envelopes, and a drag-to-assign matrix with per-route curves and lag.
