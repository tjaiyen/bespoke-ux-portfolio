The instruments behind the atlas

Open hardware, open reduction pipelines, open data.

  • Wide-field survey

    A drift-scan camera walks the meridian nightly, so the same patch of sky is measured against itself hundreds of times a year.

  • Spectroscopic follow-up

    Anything that varies gets a spectrum within the week, because a light curve without a spectrum is a question, not a result.

  • Open reduction

    Every calibration frame and pipeline commit is public, so a result you cannot reproduce is a result we treat as unfinished.

  • Community time

    A fixed share of clear-sky hours is allocated to proposals from outside the collective, including from schools.
