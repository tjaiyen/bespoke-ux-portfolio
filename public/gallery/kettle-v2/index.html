The ritual

Four unhurried steps

Good tea rewards attention. This is the small ceremony we return to, cup after cup.

  1. 1

    Heat with intention

    Bring fresh, cold water just short of a boil — around 85–95°C for greens, a rolling boil for blacks.

  2. 2

    Measure the leaf

    One heaped teaspoon per cup. Loose leaf wants room to open, so give it space to breathe and expand.

  3. 3

    Steep and wait

    Let it rest three to five minutes. Watch the steam drift and the colour deepen. No rushing this part.

  4. 4

    Pour and pause

    Strain, cradle the cup, and take the first slow sip. This is the whole point — the pause it earns you.
