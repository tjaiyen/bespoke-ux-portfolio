THREE THINGS
IT DOES.

  • 01

    STRUCTURES EVERYTHING

    Every log line is a typed record — key, value, level, timestamp. No free-text strings. If it can't be a field, it doesn't get logged.

  • 02

    QUERIES LOCALLY

    Filter, group, and count your logs from the terminal before they ever leave the box. null where level=error and you're done.

  • 03

    SHIPS ANYWHERE

    One line-delimited JSON stream out. Pipe it to a file, a socket, or your aggregator of choice. No lock-in, no agent, no daemon.
