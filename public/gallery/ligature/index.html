01 Scattered forms — a thousand loose glyphs

Drawn for the medium

Four things we test before a face leaves the studio.

  • Hinted for real screens

    Every face is tested at 11px on the panels people own, not only on the studio display where everything looks fine.

  • Optical sizes

    Display and text cuts are drawn separately, because a headline letter shrunk to caption size is a compromise, not a size.

  • Variable axes

    Weight and optical size ship as continuous axes, so a design system can interpolate instead of stockpiling static weights.

  • Wide language support

    Latin, Greek and Cyrillic drawn together from the start, so the diacritics are designed rather than bolted on.
