01 Last year's shape, and the target ring

A shape, not a scorecard

A list of twenty findings produces no action. A shape with one obvious dent produces a plan.

  • Weighted, not averaged

    A flat mean across domains lets a strong controls score paper over a weak data score, so each axis is weighted by how much the next twelve months depend on it.

  • Evidence per score

    Every point on every axis names what was observed to justify it, because a maturity score without evidence is a consultant's opinion with a number attached.

  • The blocking axis

    Domains are not independent — weak data caps what reporting and automation can ever reach — so the assessment names the axis whose ceiling the others are hitting.

  • Re-scored, not re-written

    The same rubric is applied each year against the previous shape, so progress is visible as movement rather than as a new document.
