A few things we've built.

  • Residence · 2024 · Coastal

    House on the Terrace

    A concrete pavilion cut into a hillside, opening to the sea through a single unbroken aperture. Interior volumes follow the slope, so the horizon is always level with the eye.

  • Cultural · 2023 · Civic

    The Quiet Gallery

    A small municipal gallery organised around a top-lit central void. Circulation is a slow spiral; daylight is the only ornament, moving across raw plaster through the day.

  • Interior · 2022 · Workspace

    Meridian Studio Fit-out

    Our own workspace: a single long room, one material palette, and furniture reduced to the essential. A working proof that restraint is a form of generosity.

Studio Ethos

We design by subtraction.

Good space asks for little and gives a great deal. We work slowly, in close collaboration with clients and makers, removing everything that does not serve light, use, and calm.

  • Material honesty

    Concrete, stone, timber and glass, left to be themselves. No finish that pretends to be another.

  • Proportion first

    Rooms are sized to the body and the light before they are sized to the brief. Scale is where feeling begins.

  • Built to last

    We design for decades, not seasons — details that age gracefully and repair without waste.
