01 Three streams — invoice, order, receipt

Three documents or it does not pay

Every control here exists because the alternative already happened to somebody.

  • Price, quantity, both

    A match is not a vendor-name lookup; the invoiced unit price and quantity are each held against the order and the receipt, and either one out of band stops the payment.

  • Duplicates by fingerprint

    The same invoice re-sent with a new number is caught on vendor, amount and date rather than on the number the sender controls.

  • Receipts, not promises

    Goods received is the only one of the three documents a supplier cannot write, which is exactly why it is the one that has to be in the match.

  • Exceptions with an owner

    A blocked payment routes to a named person with the reason attached, because a queue nobody owns becomes a queue somebody overrides.
