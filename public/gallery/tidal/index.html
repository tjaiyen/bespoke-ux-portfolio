01 Raw seawater — salt and sediment in suspension

An instrument for the ocean

Every reading is turned into a decision a plant operator can act on today.

  • Live salinity mapping

    Thousands of in-line sensors sample conductivity continuously, so a drift in feedwater chemistry surfaces in minutes, not on the next lab report.

  • Membrane foresight

    Fouling is predicted from pressure differentials before throughput falls, turning an emergency shutdown into a scheduled clean.

  • Energy per litre

    Recovery ratios are tuned against live energy pricing, because the cheapest litre is the one that never needed the extra pass.

  • Brine accountability

    Discharge plumes are modelled against the receiving environment, so the outfall is defensible to a regulator and to a coastline.
