What goes into a length

Four decisions, made before a single pick is thrown.

  • Fibre first

    Flax and long-staple cotton chosen for how they age, because cloth that looks best on day one is rarely the cloth you keep.

  • Natural dyeing

    Indigo vats and madder baths maintained in the studio, so colour arrives with the depth and variation only a live vat gives.

  • Dressed by hand

    Every warp is measured, sleyed and tied on by hand — three days of work before the weaving itself begins.

  • Woven to order

    Lengths are woven against a commission rather than to stock, which is why the waiting list is honest about its length.
