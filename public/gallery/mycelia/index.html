01 Inoculation — one thread finds the substrate

A material with a growth curve

Expanded polystyrene takes moments to make and centuries to leave. This is the other trade.

  • Nine-day grow cycle

    Substrate is inoculated, packed into a mould and left in the dark, and the network does the forming — no press, no tooling, and a mould change costs days rather than months.

  • Fed on local waste

    We grow on hemp hurd and crop residue sourced within a short radius, because shipping agricultural waste long distances would undo the whole point of using it.

  • Home-compostable

    It breaks down in a domestic compost heap in weeks, without an industrial facility — the qualifier that quietly makes most compostable packaging landfill.

  • Protective as EPS

    Independently tested against expanded polystyrene for drop and compression at equivalent density, because a sustainable material that fails the drop test just ships breakage instead.
