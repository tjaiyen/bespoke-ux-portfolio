01 Scattered singles — no formation, no draft

Coordination, not dispatch

Central dispatch degrades the moment reality stops matching the plan. Local rules do not.

  • Emergent routing

    Each vehicle solves against its own neighbours and constraints, so a closed road re-shapes the fleet in seconds instead of invalidating a plan computed at 4am.

  • Live re-forming

    Add a stop, lose a driver, or take a vehicle off the road and the remaining fleet closes the gap on its own — no re-run, no dispatcher on the phone.

  • Explainable decisions

    Every routing choice comes with the constraints that produced it, because a driver who cannot see why will simply drive the way they always have.

  • Reads your telematics

    We ingest the feeds you already pay for rather than asking you to fit new hardware, which means a pilot takes a fortnight instead of a quarter.
