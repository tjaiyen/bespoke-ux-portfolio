Unhurried

The ritual

Good tea asks for a little patience. Here is how we brew ours.

  1. Warm the pot

    Rinse your teapot with hot water so the leaves meet an even, welcoming heat.

  2. Measure by hand

    One rounded teaspoon of loose leaf per cup — a touch more if you like it bold.

  3. Let it drift

    Pour just-off-boil water and wait. Three to five minutes while the steam rises and the leaves unfurl.

  4. Pour and pause

    Strain, breathe in, and take the first slow sip. The rest of the day can wait.
