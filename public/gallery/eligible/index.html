The exclusions a certificate has to survive

A borrowing base is not an inventory report. It is an inventory report minus everything the lender will not count.

  • PACA outranks the bank

    An unpaid produce grower holds a federal trust claim that sits ahead of a secured lender, so that value leaves the base entirely — and it is the carve-out most often missed, because nothing in the ledger flags it.

  • Aged and slow-moving

    Stock past its eligibility window stops counting on a date, not on a judgement, so the certificate is recomputed nightly rather than argued monthly.

  • Consignment is not yours

    Goods held on consignment sit in your warehouse and on someone else's balance sheet; counting them is the fastest way to lose a lender's trust in the whole certificate.

  • Traceable to the lot

    Every excluded dollar names the lot, the reason and the rule, because a base a field examiner cannot re-derive is a base that gets discounted.
