The collections

Four studies in restraint

  • Lumière

    Solitaire rings in brushed 18-carat gold, each holding a single old-cut diamond chosen for its warmth rather than its size.

  • Ondine

    Fluid necklaces that trace the collarbone — hand-linked chain punctuated by seed pearls and pale aquamarine.

  • Braise

    Signet and band rings in a deep, ember-toned gold, engraved to order with a mark of your choosing.

  • Silence

    Unadorned earrings and cuffs — pure metal, softly polished, made for those who prefer the form to speak alone.

The atelier

One bench, one maker, one piece at a time

We keep production deliberately small. Every commission begins with a conversation and a pencil drawing, and passes through the hands of a single goldsmith from the first cut to the final polish.

Materials
Recycled 18-carat gold and responsibly sourced, individually certified stones.
Timeframe
Six to ten weeks for a bespoke commission, from first sketch to hallmark.
Guarantee
Lifetime care — cleaning, re-polishing and resizing at no charge, always.
