Rhythm, not rules

Sleep advice fails when it prescribes a clock time to a body running on a different one.

  • Phase before schedule

    We estimate your circadian phase first and set timings from it, because telling a late chronotype to sleep at ten is prescribing insomnia and calling it discipline.

  • Light as the lever

    Timed light exposure shifts a rhythm far more reliably than willpower does, so the programme is built around when you see light rather than when you lie down.

  • No sleep score

    You are not shown a nightly grade. Scoring sleep reliably makes it worse — the anxiety about the number becomes the thing keeping you awake.

  • Clinician escalation

    Signals that look like apnoea or a circadian disorder are routed to a clinician rather than coached around, because an app is the wrong place for those to be managed.
