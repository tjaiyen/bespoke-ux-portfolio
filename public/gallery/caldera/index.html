Firm power from a cooling rock

Geothermal's advantage is not that it is renewable. It is that it does not care what the weather is doing.

  • Resource proving

    We drill slim-hole exploration wells before committing to a field, because a reservoir that models well and flows badly has bankrupted better developers than us.

  • Binary-cycle plant

    Working fluid takes the heat and the brine goes straight back down the injection well, which keeps the reservoir pressurised and the dissolved minerals underground.

  • Induced-seismicity limits

    Injection pressure is bounded by a traffic-light protocol agreed with the regulator before the first well, not renegotiated after the first tremor.

  • Ninety-plus capacity factor

    A field runs at over ninety percent availability year-round, which is the number that actually lets a grid retire a gas peaker rather than idle one.
