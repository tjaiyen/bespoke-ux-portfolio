01 Raw intake — one hundred kilos, all of it paid for

Every split has a price

Yield loss is usually reported as a percentage. A percentage has never once been posted to a ledger.

  • Trim is revenue, if you sell it

    By-product streams carry a real recoverable value, and a plant that books them as loss is under-reporting both its yield and its margin.

  • Shrink is not trim

    Material that simply disappears behaves differently from material that leaves as a saleable stream, and blending them into one yield number hides a control problem inside an operations one.

  • Rework costs twice

    A rework kilo consumes line time, labour and energy a second time, so it is priced at its true cost rather than at the value of the material alone.

  • Standard vs actual yield

    The gap between the yield the standard assumes and the yield the line achieves is a usage variance, and it belongs in the cost roll rather than in a shift report.
