Work upstream of the problem

Almost every flood and drought problem is cheaper to solve above the town than in it.

  • Slow the flow

    Leaky woody dams, re-meandered channels and restored floodplain hold water in the upper catchment, so the peak arrives lower and later than the concrete downstream was designed for.

  • Soil as reservoir

    A percent of soil organic matter holds a meaningful depth of rainfall across a catchment, which makes farm soil health a flood intervention as much as an agricultural one.

  • Gauged, not asserted

    We instrument before we intervene and publish the hydrographs either way, because restoration is full of confident claims and short on before-and-after gauging.

  • Landowner-first

    Nothing happens without the farmers who own the ground. Schemes designed around them last; schemes imposed on them are quietly undone within five years.
