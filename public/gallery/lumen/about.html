Our story

A studio built on restraint

We started Lumen because the best products we admired had one thing in common: nothing extra. That absence is what we practice.

How we got here

Lumen began in 2015 as a two-person practice above a bookshop, taking on the projects larger agencies passed over — the intricate, unglamorous interfaces that real people use every day. We found we preferred them.

Over eleven years we stayed deliberately small. Six designers, no account managers, no handoff theatre. When you work with Lumen you work with the people who draw the pixels and write the specs. That closeness is the whole point.

We believe good design is mostly subtraction: removing steps, removing doubt, removing the noise between a person and what they came to do. Everything we ship is measured against that idea.

What we hold to

Principles that guide the work

  • Clarity firstIf a screen needs a manual, it is not finished. We design for the moment of use.
  • Small on purposeWe take fewer clients so each one gets our full attention, start to finish.
  • Systems, not screensWe leave teams with durable foundations, not one-off deliverables.
  • Accessible by defaultInclusive design is not a phase at the end — it shapes every decision.
