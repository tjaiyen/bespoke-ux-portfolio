Case studies

  • Healthcare2024

    Verda — clinical notes without the clutter

    A ground-up design system for a clinician tool, letting nurses capture notes in half the taps and giving the engineering team a component library they still ship on today.

  • Developer tools2024

    Forge — a CI dashboard people actually read

    We turned a wall of logs into a legible, scannable status surface, so teams spot failing builds in seconds instead of scrolling for minutes.

  • Education2023

    Atlas — mapping a learner's whole journey

    A wayfinding redesign for an online school, replacing a maze of menus with a single clear path from enrolment to graduation.

  • Marketplace2022

    Reel & Root — commerce with a sense of place

    Brand and interface work for an artisan marketplace, balancing warmth and speed so small makers could sell without losing their voice.
