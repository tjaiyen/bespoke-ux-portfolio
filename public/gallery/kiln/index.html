How a piece is made

Four stages, none of which can be hurried.

  • Thrown by hand

    Every form is raised on the wheel from a single ball of stoneware, so no two rims land in exactly the same place.

  • Trimmed green

    Once leather-hard the foot is turned by hand, giving the piece its balance and the underside worth turning over to see.

  • Glazed in small batches

    Glazes are mixed from raw materials in the studio, so a batch carries the character of the season it was made in.

  • Fired to cone 10

    A slow reduction firing over eighteen hours, cooled just as slowly — which is where the celadon finds its depth.
