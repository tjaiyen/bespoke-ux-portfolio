A house built on scarcity

Arid botanicals are slow, sparse and easy to ruin. That is precisely why they are worth composing with.

  • Named provenance

    Every material lists its cooperative and harvest year, because 'sustainably sourced' with no name attached to it is a claim that costs nothing to make.

  • Harvest ceilings

    We buy to an agreed annual ceiling set by the harvesters, so a good year for us never becomes a bad decade for a slow-growing stand of frankincense.

  • Composed, not layered

    Each fragrance is built as a single accord rather than assembled from stock bases, which is slower, more expensive, and the entire reason to run a house at all.

  • Full disclosure

    The complete material list ships with the bottle — allergens, naturals and synthetics alike — because secrecy in perfumery mostly protects the margin, not the art.
