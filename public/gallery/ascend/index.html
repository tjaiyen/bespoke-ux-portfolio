How the game works

Three simple moves take you from day one to the summit.

    Set your daily quest

    Pick the habits you want to build. Each one becomes a quest with its own goal, difficulty, and reward.

  2. 2

    Check in to build a streak

    Complete a quest each day to grow your streak. Miss a day and you slide — momentum is the whole game.

    Level up & reach the summit

    Streaks unlock badges, boost your rank, and inch you closer to the peak. Every climb has a summit to conquer.
