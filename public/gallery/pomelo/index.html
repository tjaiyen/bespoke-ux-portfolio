The story behind the fizz

Pomelo started in a tiny kitchen with a soda siphon, a crate of ugly-but-delicious citrus a farmer couldn't sell, and a stubborn belief that soda didn't have to be a guilty secret.

We still make it the slow way: press the fruit that day, sweeten it gently with cane sugar, and carbonate in batches you could count on two hands. No concentrates, no mystery flavor codes, no neon dye — just fruit that had a very good week.

Bright color, honest labels, and a fizz loud enough to hear across the room. That's the whole plan.
