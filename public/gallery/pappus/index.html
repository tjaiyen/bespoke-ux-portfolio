01 A head of seed, still holding on

A library, not a shop

Seed bought every year stays generic. Seed saved every year becomes local.

  • Free to borrow

    Nothing costs anything. Returning seed is encouraged rather than required, because a library that turns away the people who had a bad season is not a library.

  • Locally adapted

    Each generation grown and saved here gets a little better suited to this soil and this season length, which is something no packet off a rack can offer.

  • Open-pollinated only

    We stock varieties that come true from saved seed, so what you bring back is actually the thing you borrowed rather than a disappointing second generation.

  • Seed-saving taught

    Isolation distances, drying and viability are covered in short sessions each spring, because most seed returned to libraries fails simply for want of being dried properly.
