Pre-position, don't react

Restoration time is decided before the storm lands, by where the trucks already are.

  • Span-level risk

    Failure probability is computed per span from conductor age, span length, vegetation encroachment and forecast gust — not averaged across a feeder that is thirty kilometres long.

  • Crew pre-staging

    Crews and materials are positioned against predicted damage the evening before, which is the single largest lever on restoration time and costs nothing but a decision.

  • Lightning attachment modelling

    Strike density is combined with shielding failure rates to find the structures that will actually take the hit, rather than the ones that took it last time.

  • Post-event learning

    Every real outage is scored against what we predicted, and the misses are what tune the model — a forecast that is never graded is a forecast nobody should trust.
