What the practice is

Four ideas, and deliberately nothing else.

  • Paced, not prescribed

    You set the rhythm and the app follows, because a breath imposed at someone else's tempo is just another instruction.

  • No streaks

    Nothing is counted and nothing is lost by missing a day, which is the point of a practice you can return to.

  • Works offline

    Every session is on the device. No account, no sync, and nothing about your breathing leaves your phone.

  • Ends deliberately

    Sessions finish rather than autoplay into the next thing, so the calm belongs to you instead of to a session length.
