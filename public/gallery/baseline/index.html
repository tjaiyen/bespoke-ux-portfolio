Variance you can act on

A red indicator at the end of a programme is not control. It is a post-mortem.

  • Earned value, honestly claimed

    Percent complete is taken from objective work-package milestones rather than from an engineer's estimate, because the alternative is a schedule that stays at 90% for a year.

  • Independent EAC

    The estimate at completion is recomputed from cumulative CPI as well as from the control account manager's forecast, and the two are shown side by side when they disagree.

  • Variance thresholds that fire

    Cost and schedule variance breaches raise a control-account explanation with an owner and a date, rather than a colour on a chart nobody has to answer for.

  • Traceable to the WBS

    Every dollar of variance walks back to a work package, a control account and a charge number, which is what makes the analysis survive a customer review.
