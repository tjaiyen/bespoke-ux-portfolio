Built for the duty cycle

Storage is judged over decades of cycles, not on a spec sheet.

  • Cycle-aware dispatch

    Each charge decision is priced against the degradation it causes, so the asset is not spent chasing a marginal arbitrage.

  • Thermal discipline

    Cell temperature is held in a narrow band, which is the single largest determinant of how many cycles you actually get.

  • Grid services stacking

    Frequency response and capacity are layered on the same asset, because one revenue stream rarely pays for a battery.

  • Second-life routing

    Cells retiring from grid duty are graded and routed to lower-demand applications rather than straight to recycling.
