Unhurried by design

The ritual

Good tea rewards attention. Four small steps turn a drink into a pause.

  1. 1

    Warm the pot

    Rinse your teapot with hot water so the first steep stays even and the leaves open gently.

  2. 2

    Measure the leaf

    One rounded teaspoon per cup. Loose leaf wants room to swirl, so give it space to breathe.

  3. 3

    Mind the water

    Greens near 80°C, blacks and herbals at a rolling boil. Temperature is the quiet difference-maker.

  4. 4

    Let it drift

    Steep three to five minutes, watch the steam rise, and breathe. The waiting is part of the cup.
