01 Day 1 — everything starts at once

Shorten the chain, not the list

Every close project starts by making a list of forty tasks. The list is the problem.

  • Dependencies, honestly

    The chain is built from what genuinely blocks what, not from the order tasks appear on the checklist, and those two are almost never the same sequence.

  • Slack is the answer

    A task with three days of slack cannot shorten the close no matter how much it is optimised, so effort is directed at the eleven tasks that can.

  • Waiting is a task

    Time spent waiting for a bank file or a subledger to post is on the clock like everything else, and it is frequently the longest single block on the path.

  • Measured every month

    The path moves as the business changes, so it is recomputed each close rather than fixed once in a project that finished two years ago.
