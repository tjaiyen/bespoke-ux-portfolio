01 Locked lattice — nine centuries of stillness

One glacier, one season, one run

Scarcity here is not a marketing decision. It is a melt rate.

  • Single-season runs

    Each bottling is dated to the melt window it came from, because a warm July and a cold one do not produce the same water and we will not blend them to pretend otherwise.

  • Untouched mineralogy

    The water arrives already filtered by nine hundred years of compression, so we add nothing and strip nothing — the profile on the label is the profile in the ice.

  • Measured extraction

    Draw is capped at a fraction of the annual melt and audited by an independent glaciologist, because a source you exhaust is a source you only sell once.

  • Returnable glass

    Bottles come back, are sterilised locally, and go out again. Shipping empty glass across a continent to be melted down was never the sustainable option.
