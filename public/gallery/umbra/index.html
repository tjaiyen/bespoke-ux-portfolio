01 First contact — the disc takes its first bite

The logistics are the expedition

Everyone knows the date years ahead. Almost nothing else about an eclipse is easy.

  • Mobile to the weather

    We hold vehicles and a decision window until the morning of, because a fixed camp under the one cloud in the region is the most expensive way to miss an eclipse.

  • Centre-line placement

    Positioning is planned to the centre line, where totality runs longest — the difference between the edge and the middle of the path is most of the experience.

  • Astronomer-led, small

    Groups stay small enough that the astronomer is answering your question rather than addressing a coach, which is the whole reason to travel with anyone at all.

  • Honest about failure

    Cloud is a real possibility and we say so in writing with the historical rates for each site, rather than selling certainty we cannot deliver.
