Two axes, one decision

Reporting emissions is a compliance exercise. Putting them next to margin is a management one.

  • Per unit, not per site

    A site total moves with volume and tells you nothing about mix, so intensity is computed per case and held against that case's own contribution margin.

  • Allocated like overhead

    Shared energy and refrigeration load are allocated to products on a defensible driver, using the same discipline a cost accountant already applies to indirect cost.

  • Volume is the third axis

    A high-intensity product at trivial volume is a rounding error, and a modest-intensity product at scale is the whole footprint, so bubble area carries the volume.

  • Auditable inputs

    Emission factors are versioned and sourced, because a number that cannot be re-derived next year is a number that cannot be reported against a target.
