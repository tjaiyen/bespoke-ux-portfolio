01 Year one — a seedling and a survival curve

Measured, not promised

Every claim traces back to a plot someone actually walked.

  • Species-level survival

    Survival is tracked per species per plot, because a headline survival rate hides the three species doing all the dying.

  • Canopy from orbit

    Multispectral passes measure closure against a control plot, so growth is compared to what the land would have done anyway.

  • Soil carbon sampling

    Physical cores on a fixed grid, because below-ground carbon is most of the story and the least often measured.

  • Twenty-year ledger

    Each planting keeps an append-only record — what was planted, what died, what was replanted, and what it cost.
